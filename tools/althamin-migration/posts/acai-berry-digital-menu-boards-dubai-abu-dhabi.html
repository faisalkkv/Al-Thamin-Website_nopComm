The solution: LG webOS digital menu screens run by Screendrive

We installed LG webOS commercial displays and integrated them with Screendrive, our digital signage software. Screendrive runs on the displays themselves, so each menu screen plays its content without a separate media player behind the screen.

  • Four-screen menu wall at every outlet — 12 screens in total.
  • One dashboard for Mirdif Avenue Mall, Dubai Design District and Al Qana.
  • Menus, prices and promotions updated remotely and scheduled by outlet, screen and time of day.
  • No extra hardware behind the screens: the LG webOS displays run Screendrive directly.

How long does it take to install digital menu boards in a Dubai mall?
