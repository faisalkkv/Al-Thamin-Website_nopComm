Every day, residents, office tenants and hotel guests spend time waiting for and riding lifts. In Dubai’s towers that time adds up — and a well-placed lift screen turns it into useful information for the building and a new income stream for the owner.

Lift screens are digital displays inside elevators and lift lobbies. For real estate they replace paper notices, keep residents informed and earn advertising revenue; for hotels they welcome guests, promote restaurants, spa and events, and share information in Arabic and English — all updated remotely from one dashboard.

Why lift screens work: a captive audience

A lift ride is one of the few moments when people stand still with nothing to do. Unlike a poster on a corridor wall, a lift screen sits at eye level, shows fresh content and can change by time of day — so messages actually get seen in busy towers in Dubai Marina, JLT, Business Bay and Downtown Dubai.

How it works: one dashboard for every lift

The screens run on Screendrive digital signage software. Building managers and hotel teams upload notices, adverts and promotions once and schedule them per tower, per lift and per time of day. Content keeps playing if the internet connection drops, and Arabic and English layouts are supported.

Planning lift screens: a quick checklist

  • Placement: inside the lift cabin, in the lift lobby — or both.
  • Screen size and orientation to suit the space and viewing distance.
  • Approval from the building owner, owners’ association or hotel management.
  • Power and network (Wi-Fi or SIM) at each location.
  • Content owner: who updates the screens, and how often.
